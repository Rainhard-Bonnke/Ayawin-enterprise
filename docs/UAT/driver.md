# Driver UAT

## Setup
- Sign in as Driver and use an assigned test vehicle, route, and disposable delivery data.
- Confirm the account is not associated with a live route.

## Journey
- Review assigned deliveries and verify customer, item, and quantities.
- Record proof of delivery or an exception using the supported workflow.
- Submit a driver stock count and route closure with declared collections and required notes.
- Verify the submitted count/closure appears with the expected pending-review status.

## Authorization checks
- Verify the Driver cannot approve their own route closure, post customer receipts, change sales prices, or access user administration.
- Verify route and vehicle data cannot be changed outside the assigned workflow.

## Result
- Record tester, date, company, build, evidence links, pass/fail, and defects. Reconcile test vehicle stock after completion.
