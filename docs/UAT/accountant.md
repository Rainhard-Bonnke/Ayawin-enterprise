# Accountant UAT

## Setup
- Sign in as Accountant in the UAT company with a disposable open accounting period.
- Prepare test invoice, receipt, supplier bill, bank statement, and payroll remittance records as applicable.

## Journey
- Create and post a customer invoice; record and allocate a customer receipt.
- Reconcile a test bank statement and verify the statement difference reaches zero before completion.
- Review VAT evidence/return preparation and statutory payroll remittances; verify figures against source transactions.
- Review accounts payable and receivable aging and confirm the totals reconcile to the registers.

## Authorization checks
- Verify the Accountant cannot approve an item they prepared when maker-checker separation applies.
- Verify the role cannot administer users, revoke other users' sessions, or certify production integrations by default.

## Result
- Record tester, date, company, build, evidence links, pass/fail, and defects. Do not submit statutory or provider filings from UAT.
