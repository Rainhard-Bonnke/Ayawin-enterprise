# Store Manager UAT

## Setup
- Sign in as Store Manager and prepare test stock in a non-production warehouse.
- Ensure test items cover tracked lots, quarantine, and ordinary stock where applicable.

## Journey
- Review stock on hand, stock movements, and reorder alerts.
- Receive a test purchase order, transfer stock between warehouses, and confirm source and destination balances.
- Record a stock count or adjustment with evidence; verify the resulting movement and audit record.
- Place a test lot into quarantine and complete an authorized disposition.

## Authorization checks
- Verify the role cannot create or post supplier payments, approve customer receipts, or administer users by default.
- Verify any approval-required adjustment or disposition is blocked for its preparer.

## Result
- Record tester, date, company, build, evidence links, pass/fail, and defects. Reconcile test stock after completion.
