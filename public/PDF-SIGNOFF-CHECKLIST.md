# PDF template sign-off checklist

Use this with finance and the client before go-live. Generate each PDF from staging with **live company details** filled in Settings → Company.

## Documents to review

| # | Document | How to generate | Sign-off |
|---|----------|-----------------|----------|
| 1 | Tax invoice | Sales → Invoices → open invoice → **Download PDF** | ☐ Layout, KRA PIN, VAT/excise lines, totals |
| 2 | Receipt (paid invoice) | Same invoice after payment → **Download Receipt** | ☐ Payment reference, amount, verification hash |
| 3 | Payslip | HR → Payroll run (posted) → **Download payslip** | ☐ PAYE, NHIF, NSSF, Housing Levy, net pay |
| 4 | Verification certificate | Invoices → preview → verification tools | ☐ QR and hash verify on portal |

## Company fields required (Settings → Company)

- Legal name and trading name  
- **Tax registration number** (KRA PIN on PDFs)  
- Address, phone, email (footer block)  
- Base currency **KES**

## Notes

- Proforma invoices must be watermarked or labelled **PROFORMA — NOT A TAX INVOICE** (confirm in PDF).  
- After sign-off, freeze template changes until post go-live review.  
- File signed PDF samples in your QMS or project folder.
