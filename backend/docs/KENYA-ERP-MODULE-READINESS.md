# Kenya ERP module readiness

Audit date: 19 October 2026. This is a code and workflow audit of the current repository, not a certification that the product complies with every customer's statutory, accounting, or industry requirements. A navigation item is not counted as a complete workflow unless the UI and API support it.

For the target operating model, module boundaries, cross-module workflows, AI policy, and delivery sequence, see [AYAWIN-ERP-OPERATING-MODEL.md](./AYAWIN-ERP-OPERATING-MODEL.md).

## Payroll changes in this audit

- Replaced the retired NHIF deduction on new payroll runs with SHIF at 2.75% of gross monthly pay, minimum KES 300. Legacy NHIF values remain on historical payslips and are separately reportable.
- Applied the published NSSF Year 4 schedule from February 2026: 6% employee and employer contributions, Tier I to KES 9,000 and total pensionable-pay cap KES 108,000. Payslips retain employee Tier I/II and employer totals plus a rules snapshot.
- Applied KRA's current monthly PAYE bands and KES 2,400 resident personal relief. Taxable pay now accounts for employee SHIF and employee Affordable Housing Levy, with supported caps for declared pension, post-retirement medical fund, mortgage-interest deductions, and insurance relief.
- Added the employer's matching NSSF and Affordable Housing Levy to employer cost and payroll journal expense/liability totals.
- Blocked recalculation of a payroll month that has already been posted.

These rules were checked against KRA's [PAYE guidance](https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/paye) (bands, relief, allowable deductions, levies and deadlines), KRA's [eTIMS FAQ](https://www.kra.go.ke/helping-tax-payers/faqs/learn-about-etims), the [Social Health Insurance Regulations](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2024/49/eng%402024-09-20/source) (2.75%, KES 300 minimum), SHA's [employer remittance notice](https://new.sha.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PUBLIC_NOTICE_ON_EMPLOYER_REMITTENCES.pdf), and NSSF's [Year 4 2026 employer notice](https://gaa.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-02/Notice%20To%20Employers%20%E2%80%94%20Year%204%20%282026%29%20NSSF%20Contribution%20Rates%20.pdf). Payroll rule settings must remain versioned by effective date; future statutory changes must not silently rewrite historical payslips.

## Recommended implementation order

1. Complete inventory lot balances, FEFO issue/transfer allocation, stocktakes, valuation, in-transit handling, and recall/return workflows. See [inventory controls and roadmap](./INVENTORY-CONTROLS-AND-ROADMAP.md).
2. Complete purchase three-way matching, supplier credits, remittance advice, and supplier statement reconciliation.
3. Complete tax-point and excise handling, VAT input recovery checks, tax-period reporting, customer refunds/statement allocation and collections. Customer credit notes and stock returns now have an internal maker-checker posting workflow; KRA eTIMS submission remains unconnected.
4. Add bank reconciliation and period-close controls to finance.
5. Complete employee lifecycle, leave accrual, payroll approvals and statutory export/reconciliation.
6. Implement certified eTIMS, Daraja, email/SMS, and report delivery connectors with credentials and callback verification.
7. Close fixed-asset lifecycle and administration/privacy controls.

For each stage, reconcile opening balances and representative end-to-end transactions with the accounts and operations owners before enabling production posting.
